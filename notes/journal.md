# Journal

One entry per graded bet, newest day first, written by the results checker as soon as a bet is graded. Each line: result, bet, the angle and confidence, the final score, whether the thesis was right, and a lesson only when there is a real one.

Format:

## YYYY-MM-DD
- WON | Bruins moneyline (NHL, goalie, High) | Bruins 4, Rangers 2 | Thesis right: backup allowed 4 on 22 shots. Closed 2.05 (CLV +9%).
- LOST | Over 6.5 goals (NHL, model, Medium) | Kings 2, Ducks 1 | Thesis wrong: both starters stopped 95%+. Lesson: no overs with two hot starters.
