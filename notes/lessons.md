# Lessons

What the record has taught so far. The morning run reads this first; the weekly review rewrites it. Keep it short: rules worth following, each with the evidence behind it.

## Standing rules

- **A stats-model gap against Pinnacle is not a reason to bet.** The first 18 bets were chosen that way (Oct 6–12): 10–8, about what the odds implied, and both picks with a closing line closed about 3% worse than we bet. The model is a map of where to look, not a decision.
- **Bet the list early.** On Oct 10 most of the morning's edges were gone by 3 PM: the prices moved against us during the day. John should place the list as soon as it posts.
- **Never bet after the start.** Live odds are a different market from the one we priced.
- **Big road spreads on teams that can't score are traps.** Titans +7.5 at Houston (Oct 11) lost 26–3; a defense can't cover for an offense that can't move the ball.
- **Soccer 1X2 longshots (the draw at 4.55, Espanyol at 2.60) went 0–2.** Prefer goals markets and handicaps where the read is about how the game will be played.
