How it works

How the daily list gets made, and how the system learns from every result.

Every morning at 7:54 the analyst run handicaps the day's games and posts up to 10 bets by 10:00. At 3:53 PM it rechecks the prices. Every hour from 10 AM to 2 AM another run grades finished games, writes a short review of each bet, and updates this site. Every Sunday night a review run studies the week and updates the rules the analyst follows.

1. Read the record first

Before looking at a single game, the analyst reads its own scorecard (how every bet type, sport and angle has done) and the reviews of recent bets. It leans into what's been working and stops doing what hasn't.

2. Find the games worth studying

It pulls every game on the slate with odds from every book, including Pinnacle, the sharpest sportsbook, and gathers the day's news: injuries, confirmed starters, goalies and pitchers, weather. It then picks the 8 to 15 games where something has changed or the price looks off.

3. Handicap each game like a pro

For each game it works out who's actually playing, how the matchup plays out, the situation (rest, travel, motivation, weather) and how much of recent form is luck. It writes a short game script and its own probability for the bet before looking at Stake's odds, then argues the other side. If the case against is better, it passes.

4. Where the edges are

  • News the market is slow on: a confirmed backup goalie, a scratched pitcher, a player ruled out after the line was set.
  • Knock-on effects: when a star sits, his teammates get more minutes and shots, and their prop lines lag behind.
  • Situations: back-to-backs, long road trips, travel, teams resting starters, letdown and lookahead spots.
  • Weather: strong wind pushes football and baseball scoring down.
  • Overreaction: the public overrates the last game it saw and the most popular teams.
  • Stake's own mistakes: prices Stake hasn't updated yet, caught by the error finder.

5. Check the price

Being right only pays if the odds are good enough. Each bet needs at least a 3% edge at Stake's price and odds of 1.30 or more. Its "take at" price is the lowest odds that still clear that bar: below it, skip the bet. The analyst's read is checked against Pinnacle, and if it differs by a lot it has to name the specific fact the market hasn't priced.

6. The list

Up to 10 bets, best first, each with a one-line reason and a confidence level (High or Medium). At most two bets per game, and never two bets that win or lose together. The Bankroll page turns each one into a dollar amount from your balance and your money plan.

7. Learning from every bet

When a bet is graded, the results run writes what happened and whether the reasoning held up. The closing price is recorded too: when the market moves toward our bet before the game, that's the clearest sign the read was right, win or lose. Every Sunday the weekly review turns those notes into updated rules.

Under the hood

The analyst's playbook is ANALYST.md. Its scorecard, lessons and bet reviews are in the notes folder. The pricing engine and data tools are in the model folder.
